Deliberately temporary — the palisade, tents, and tarp-covered smithy are all built for quick relocation as the colony pushes deeper into the caldera. The cleared dirt inside the perimeter is because the soil here is too recently turned for anything to grow back. Four service nodes cover the full expedition economy: **Physician's Tent** — treatment, medical hires for expeditions **Butcher's Hut** — bounties in salt cubes and iron keys, Survivalist hires, kill-location flags for large creatures **Smith's Tarp** — gear repair, squire and porter hires **The Stone Structure** — map data purchase, Scholar hires (paid on survival, not contract) The Stone Structure itself is pre-Riftstone — the same make as the Black Stone, and from its top the Collapsed Tower is visible far to the west. The party may not connect these dots early. The Ferry Landing is where expeditions begin and end. PCs return between expeditions for rest, healing, bounties, trade, and news.
