The outpost mines sulfur from the sinkhole's geothermal deposits and processes guano into saltpeter. Combined with charcoal from fallen trees, these are the three ingredients for gunpowder — the Batkin's primary military advantage.</p><p><strong>Cut off.</strong> The caved-in tunnel was their supply line back to the main Batkin territory. The Mudback goblins collapsed it intentionally, severing this outpost. The Batkin cannot call for reinforcements or ship their gunpowder stockpile out.</p><p><strong>Undead as passive defense.</strong> Batkin don't clear undead — they roost high where the zombies can't reach. The corpse sea on the cave floor was once a much larger horde. Cut off from resupply, most have fully rotted. Only 6 functional walkers remain at the work stations, plus crawlers hidden in the corpse piles and shamblers stumbling through the mess. Zombie composition: Ork Zombies and Mudback Goblin Zombies. The goblin zombies are notably old — Mudback goblins normally take their dead back to the spawning pool before mycelial conversion, so their skulls stay alive long after death and bodies take much longer to turn.</p><p><strong>The bat town.</strong> Three adobe domes on the cave ceiling. The Production Dome is largest and where the hoist arrives — full of gunpowder processing vats. The Sleeping Dome and Food Storage Dome are on the far side, each guarded by a mounted gun outside the door. The food vats are filled with blood. 3 Vampiric Bats remain alive. 3 Stalker Bats roost here at night, 6 during the day, hanging from the metal rods between domes. All dome interiors are difficult terrain from storage clutter.</p><p><strong>Armaments:</strong></p><p>Mounted Gun (×3): 30m range, 2AP fire, 3AP reload, 3d12+Prowess Shrapnel. One at hoist top, one outside each far dome.</p><p>Batkin Bomb: 2AP throw, 4d4+4 Shrapnel, 3m radius. Stockpiled in the equipment dome.</p><p>Batkin rifles and bone armor also stored in the equipment dome.</p><p><strong>Day vs Night.</strong> During the day, 6 Stalker Bats roost in the cave. The cave is deafeningly loud with clicking and flapping. At night, most bats leave to hunt — only 3 Stalker Bats and the 3 Vampiric Bats remain. Night entry is quieter but the Batkin are home and alert.</p><p><strong>Snotling Quest Connection.</strong> The Snotling Tribe wants the Batkin dealt with — they've been raiding goblin territory. Completing this is a major snotling alliance quest payoff.</p><p><strong>Sulfur.</strong> The yellow crystals are sulfur, mined from geothermal deposits around the still water. The white crystals in the evaporation pans are saltpeter refined from guano. Both are valuable — sulfur especially so as a gunpowder component.</p><p><strong>Player choices.</strong> Kill the Batkin (snotling quest), trade with them (they're desperate and cut off), or ignore them and loot the sulfur.
