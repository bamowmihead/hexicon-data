# Berserker

**Vocation · active build target.** The party's engine/mule/scary-dog (identity epiphany 2/17: "if you were traveling with someone who said oh yea I just tank things in real life you'd want to kick them in the balls").

**Locked canon (1.0, live):** Trance tiers **Aspect / Were / Wild / Awakened / Dire** (named 6/06). Soul-eating was core (the "Gourmand's Gift" name is REJECTED); **Burn vs Subsume** — burn a subsumed soul for an immediate effect, or subsume permanently toward attunement. Growth curve deliberately slowed; attunement is intentional growth — see [[concepts/attunement]]. Animal companions = alternate slower trance-learning path. Faction pairing: Mutagenists.

**Superseded by 2.0 (designed 9/27–9/28, NOT released — the live Berserker stays as-is until 2.0 ships all at once):** soul-eating / burn vs subsume are **retired**; Were and Wild are two directions, not a ladder; **ferality** is the energy, capacity = Intuition × 10 + 20; attunement is befriend-never-kill; kingdoms give way to body plans. The whole ruling set: [[concepts/berserker-2-0-design]].

**Historical frame (March omnibus — concept canon, numbers unconfirmed):** Willpower equip cap, 5-min attunement ritual, beast-gear gating, size penalties, 9 starter trance creatures.

**In the app:** the vocation row [[vocations/berserker]] (panel: trance, energy: reagents); 12 trance cards under [[trances]]; the 2.0 shapes exist as tables (trance forms, berserk levels, mig 379) but nothing of 2.0 is live.

**Sources:** `library-omnibus-2026-03-27.md` · `library-current-state §14` · Berserker Guide 2.0 + Trance Cards (April play docs, stale frame)
