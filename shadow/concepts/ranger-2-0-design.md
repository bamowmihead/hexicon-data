# Ranger 2.0 — Design (not released)

**Status:** in design, NOT released. Ships all at once, like Berserker 2.0. Everything below is Reece's ruling (9/27–9/28) unless tagged *(Claude, pending)*. Parent: [[concepts/ranger]].

## Fantasy

- Foraging for and discovering plants; gardening them in an ever-growing garden; growing their seeds, spores and spuds instantly on the battlefield to shape it, support allies, and more.

## Energy

- **Intuition sets the limit** for Overgrowth, as it does for every vocation's energy (so Intuition isn't a dump stat).
- **Reagents grow plants.** Reagents come from processing ingredients you don't want to use directly, or as an alchemy byproduct.

## Overgrowth

- Your body doesn't disappear: it **roots in place.** You become the plants you've cultivated and can act from them.
- **Root clones:** for 1 AP, a plant version of you grows from one of your cultivated plants and starts acting. It takes on that plant's traits.
- Root clones are fragile and disposable. Dying in one deals non-lethal damage: it reduces Overgrowth and Endurance.
- Root-clone special abilities (from the plant) **cost Overgrowth.**
- The symbiote makes Overgrowth easier to achieve.
- **No loss-of-control equivalent** for the Ranger (Reece: no fantasy that would meaningfully add to the vocation).

## Living armor (the companion)

- Beast gear's Ranger equivalent: **plants grown as armor, living as a symbiote on the Ranger.** This is the Ranger's companion feature.
- It has **its own turn:** it can break off and act, or spend its turn on you.
- **Minor plants** have smaller trees and don't block you from attuning other plants (like Berserker minor beasts).
- **Late level:** it becomes a **symbiote**, strengthening you and the plant and opening a slot for another living armor over it.

## Plant relationship ladder

- **1:** find the plant and add it to your garden → you get its seeds.
- **2:** forage it, **do alchemy with it**, then **spend reagents** to start the symbiosis → it can be equipped as living armor next session. The symbiosis makes it sentient.
- **After that:** +1 point every session. The plant can die.
- Reagents can enhance the plant.

## Specialization: growth habits

- Trees & Bushes, Vines, Fungi, Mold, Algae, Lichen, Moss.

## The garden

- Limited in size; can be improved.
- **Companion planting** (like the three sisters): plants that complement each other produce more and better ingredients.
- The Ranger ends up with more ingredients: **the better alchemist,** while the Berserker is the better hands-on warrior.

## Alchemy (both Druid vocations)

- Ranger ingredients: **plants and fungi, from foraging.** Berserker ingredients: **monster and critter parts, from hunting.** Same concepts, no overlap; different ingredients, different effects.
- **Berserker unique line: mutagenics.** Changes you and gives you aspects of a creature; can be shared with friends, or combined with a trance for a hybrid creature.
- **Ranger unique line:** growth agents and seed bombs (ruled 9/28, below).
- Both also share an alchemical space, each with their own ingredients.
- Neither is limited in which bases they can use; effects lean toward certain types per vocation.

## Rulings 9/28

- **Overgrowth is produced by your plants every round** (amount varies by plant and plant level). **Intuition × 10 + 20 is the cap** on how much you can hold to spend on abilities, like activating plants' special features.
- **Ranger unique alchemy: growth agents** (affect plants directly) and **seed bombs** (plant-based effects combined with alchemical effects).
- **Vocation tree branches:** Overgrowth, Cultivation, Gardening, Alchemy.
- **Rangers get one free living armor.**
- **Only root-clone deaths hurt the Ranger.** A root clone's death also **lowers the growth** of the plant it came from, rather than damaging it.
- Build handoff: `HANDOFF_RANGER_2_0_DESIGN_TEMPLATE_2026-09-28.md`.
- **The body roots only while acting through a root clone.**
- **Plants grow a level on their own every 3 turns** (slower than before); growth can be accelerated by spending reagents, growth agents, or Overgrowth.
- **1 plant relationship point = 1 growth-habit point** (1:1, like body plans).

## Rulings 9/28 (later)

- **After combat:** Overgrowth drops to half, then drains slowly; leftover can help find forageables. **Not** usable on the garden.
- **Controlling existing plants costs Overgrowth; growing new plants doesn't** (Overgrowth can speed growth).
- **Outside combat:** gained by foraging, by being at a base with a garden or plants, and by growing plants; **capped at half capacity** outside a fight. Adventuring builds momentum.
- **Biome compatibility:** compatible (faster, more Overgrowth), neutral, hostile (slower, less).
- **Plants stay after a fight;** some become harvestable for resources and consumables.

## Open

- Parked: cross-breeding plants (Reece likes it; hard to design).
