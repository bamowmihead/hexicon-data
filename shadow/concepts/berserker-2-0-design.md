# Berserker 2.0 — Design (not released)

**Status:** in design, NOT released. Berserker 2.0 ships all at once; the live Berserker (and the Verdant Goat card built 9/27 for the 10-04 session) stays as-is until then. Everything below is Reece's ruling (9/27) unless tagged *(Claude, pending)*. Parent: [[concepts/berserker]].

## Core

- **Trancing** (wild shape) is the core feature. The Aspect / Were / Wild names stay.
- **Were and Wild are two separate directions,** not a ladder: the hybrid (keeps tools and weapons) vs the full animal.
- **Ferality** happens during combat. Raised by **feeding on living flesh** and by other things specific to the creature you're trancing into.
- **You spend ferality to trance.** You can't just spend reagents: you have to psych yourself up or do something crazy. **Too much ferality and you have no choice but to trance.**
- **You spend ferality to go berserk:** stronger, faster, crazier, more bloodthirsty. You choosing to go ape shit. You can berserk without trancing; **berserking while tranced is the Berserker's prime.**
- **Berserk is a ladder:** at the first level you still have your shit together; each level up you're more dangerous to yourself and your allies.
- **Wild forms of big creatures start small:** your first Wild form of a creature larger than you is a slightly bigger than you version, not full size. Full size is earned.
- **Soul-eating / burn vs subsume: retired.**

## Attunement (befriend, never kill)

- **Attunement 1 — Speech:** befriend the creature and stay at peace (neither kills the other). You can speak with it.
- **Attunement 2 — Aspect form:** do the creature a favour.
- **Attunement 3 — Quest:** do a quest for the creature. It will now go on expedition with you. You gain an attunement point.
- **Each session the creature survives with you, at peace:** another attunement point.
- Attunement points are spent on **that creature's attunement tree**, which works like a small specialization.

## The attunement tree (a shared template, four branches)

1. **Were** — deepen Were form.
2. **Wild** — deepen Wild form (including growing toward full size).
3. **Bond** — coordinating with your companion; includes **pack** (a pack of the creature, or a swarm for small creatures).
4. **Beast gear** — nearly identical for every creature; gated by **materials and a quest**, not attunement points. Gear improves as you invest; it grants the beast's senses, resistances and abilities without being tranced, and makes trancing into that creature cheaper or free.

- **Capstone example:** a pack of Verdant Goats; trance into Wild or Were with 1 AP and no ferality cost; enhanced forms; keep some of this trance's benefits while tranced into another creature.

## Companions

- **Charm** sets how many creatures you can bring at once (the same number you can train at once). **No limit on how many you can have bonded.**
- **The Berserker gets 1 free beast companion** on top of the Charm limit.
- **Minor beasts** (example: a falcon) have a smaller attunement list: they don't cost a slot, are limited to Aspect form, their beast gear is a charm (not a full armour set slot), and most of their tree is the Bond branch.
- Out of combat, creatures have their own skills: some are **mounts** (a pack of mounts can mount the whole party); others scout, track, stalk, keep watch, hunt, or gather resources.

## Ferality numbers

- **Capacity = Intuition × 10 + 20** (changed 9/28 from Willpower: Intuition stays the limit for every vocation's energy so it isn't a dump stat). Stages at each quarter: with a capacity of 40, stage 1 at 10, stage 2 at 20, stage 3 at 30+.
- **You can go past capacity.** Past it, you lose control (attack the nearest threat or prey).
- Before the cap you just get more powerful and are mostly a danger to yourself; **past the cap you become everyone else's problem.**

## Reagents in 2.0

- Enhance your beast companions directly (their stats and abilities).
- Increase your ferality.

## Specialization: body plans (replaces kingdoms)

- The kingdom specializations (Calderan / Goblinoid / Cavernoid / Wrangler) are **retired.**
- Specialize in **body plans**: better at were-forms, avians, aquatics, quadrupeds, arachnids, etc.
- Body-plan specialization points are **earned by improving attunements.**

## Vocation tree

- Four branches: **Berserking, Trancing, Attunement, Alchemy.**

## Attunement trees

- Almost all follow the shared template, with **their own specials branching off it.**

## Berserking and cost rules (9/27, later)

- **Berserk is a choice:** spend ferality to go up a level. **Past capacity**, you go up a berserk level immediately and again every round until ferality drops below capacity.
- **Trance cost** depends on the creature, your attunement to it, and your beast gear.
- **Ferality decays by Willpower each round** (working idea). **After combat,** once it's down to half capacity it drains 1 per hour, and leftover ferality can be spent to improve out-of-combat skills (senses, tracking, spotting ambushes, finding prey).
- **Evisceration:** consume or coat yourself in an enemy's flesh; gain ferality and deal **fear damage** to nearby enemies (Endurance only, can never wound).
- **1 attunement point = 1 body-plan point.**

## Rulings 9/28

- **Outside combat:** ferality is gained by **hunting**, a material cost (reagents), and the current trance's own ways; animal companions contribute. **Capped at half capacity** outside a fight.
- **In combat, companions feed ferality:** when a companion is injured or hurts something, you gain ferality.
- **High level:** command animals (beyond your companions) at a high ferality cost.
