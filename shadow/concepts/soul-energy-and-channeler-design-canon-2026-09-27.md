# Cosmology
- Four states on two axes. **Primal (immaterial):** anima = living, aether = dead. **Umbral (physical):** ichor = living, umbra = dead. "Primal" is the common word for anima.
- Elemental names: fire = anima, air = aether, water = ichor, earth = umbra.
- Liturgy (Reece, verbatim): "Umbral is the blood, the bones and the body, / Primal is the air in our lungs and the warmth from within which creates life. In balance these things create life something stable in an ocean of entropy." We breathe the air of dead ancestors and are held up by the bones they left.
- In the body: **anima = Endurance, ichor = wound slots, umbra = substance/bones** (durability in gear). Life is controlled burning; blood buffers fire from bone.
- **Anima exposed to air outside a body becomes aether.** Some pulls back into anima, which is why anima makes light and what gives channeling its range.
- **Free aether pulls warmth** (spirit chill, cold haunted rooms); aether held in a gem does not. Aether + anima = a spirit. Aether + umbra = death (a vessel). Ichor + umbra = undeath. Air + water is NOT life.
- Divine can't be made; it is found.
- Everything has soul, in graded density. Once-living matter holds living energy best; never-living holds dead energy. Stone is low-density like air; **metal is refined, super-dense umbra** (Inquisitors can shape it).
# Storage & economy
- Mineral gems hold aether (inert inside), flow-limited; overfilling faster than they vent makes them explode. Anima can't enter them.
- **Elementals** = gems born of aether without anima (aether + umbra). A spirit locked in a gem is NOT an elemental; it's rare, and can leak aether to chill a room. Wake it by siphoning aether and infusing anima at the same time. Golems = elementals given a body.
- **Grave gems** = solid ichor that holds anima: dual infusion. Highly valuable. Maybe carry a nascent aura, reusable by infusing anima.
- **Blood is dilute ichor**, refined into vials (common). Some creatures bleed pure ichor. Ichor pulled by a Channeler is pure; ordinary bleeding isn't. **No refined reactions from unrefined sources.**
- Ichor is easier for Inquisitors to get; aether easier for Channelers. Both are Sensate faction currency. Druid currency: a bone resource + reagents.
# Verbs, durations, range
- Verbs: **infuse, siphon, equalize.** Siphon is a direction; channeling is energy transfer. You can't siphon yourself (drawing from yourself = infusing another).
- Durations: pulse → channel → bind. Bind is a longer-term infuse/siphon/equalize, not a verb.
- **Equalize** moves two pools toward each other; channeled, it becomes a bind = a link where damage and healing are split.
- **All channeling is at range.** Touch-only umbral is an Inquisitor rule (Inquisitors can't channel).
- Starting kit: like-to-like only (anima↔anima, ichor↔ichor, umbra↔umbra). No aether at start. Cross-type reactions later.
# Dice, cost, crits
- **Dice per AP = sources drawn at once.** Skill raises the number of sources (reserve, body, gem/vial, companion spirit, enemy via siphon). Earned ability: reserve + body at once = 2 dice; drawing from your body always costs something.
- **Die size from the foci.** A foci IS the catalyst; its slots hold shapes, which the **player chooses** and can change during downtime. The **Nail** is a shareable foci: a rechargeable spell slot handed to another player to use. The Channeler's own starting foci is still unnamed.
- **Foci die ladder (9/27):** no foci = d4; Nail, Glove, Amulet = d6; Spike, Wand = d8; Staff = d10; Stave = d12.
- **Sigils still exist:** they add dice at the cost of more energy to cast.
- **Reserve = Intuition × 10 + 20** (9/27; supersedes Energy Capacity Intuition × 2 + 10). Holds anima and aether.
- **Soul Resistance tiers replace damage buckets for all soul-energy transfers**; the four energies aren't bucketed damage types. Undead take ichor at ×3. A normal wound on a guttering target refills Endurance, ending the gutter.
- Sessions: marking a session complete awards points. Dead characters go on a Memorial tab on House and Company pages; their items auto-transfer to the House inventory.
- Mixed die sizes allowed. **Willpower bonus per die** (big pools are a high-level feature; martial vocations have their own scaling; a foci sword makes the weapon part of the magic).
- Energy is spent only when the Channeler is the source. Anima from your reserve costs half the die size. **Siphon is free**, returns half of what it deals (to Endurance or reserve).
- Crits per maxed die: standard = refund to whoever paid / siphon returns more. **A foci shape replaces the standard crit; each maxed die enhances the shape again** (Chain chains again). Touch = efficiency focus.
# Runoff & aether sickness
- Spending anima refunds some aether to your pool and vice versa; the pool still depletes (refuel from gems, siphons). Capacity = how long you can run one type before swapping.
- Ichor use leaves some on the ground; anima into spilled ichor or an ichor vial makes an aura there.
- Aether building up causes **aether sickness**: mostly reversible; fully aether mummifies you, which looks like aging but isn't.
- Aether-track Channelers can enter **spirit form** fairly early: body stays behind (still ichor) as a target; return by pushing back in; if the body dies you die slowly and keep playing a while. Spirits channel (different verbs, stronger, larger dice), no weapons. Pinnacle may be moving into a husk.
# Buffs & debuffs
- Each creature has one signed deviation number per energy (0 = natural balance). Above = buffs then overdose; below = debuffs then collapse. Progressive steps.
- **Capacity = Endurance × wound slots.** Step width = capacity ÷ 7 (centre band normal); more steps can extend either way.
- **Soul Resistance** works both ways (rate of change). Tiers ×4, ×3, ×2, ×1, ×0.5, ×0.25, immune. Smoke and auras change it.
- Deviation ticks one step toward normal **each pulse (12 h)**. Flare fades once per round and must be fed. Umbra/ichor move slow, anima/aether fast.
- Ladders: anima+ raises max Endurance (Medic overheal); anima− drains, at 0 **gutter** (speed halved, any damage wounds). Ichor+ fortifies (Endurance recovery per round for a while); ichor− wounds, brittle bones. Umbra+ hardens (+1 Defense, −2 Speed per step; encased at Speed 0; suffocates over minutes; meat preserved, used for food). Umbra− lowers max Endurance, lighter, rubber-bone collapse, non-lethal. Aether+ flares (speed); siphoning aether ends a flare.
# Key target rulings
- Living: anima infuse/siphon Endurance (never wounds); ichor siphon wounds only if it takes Endurance to 0; ichor heals wounds; late: two wounds if already wounded and ichor beats max Endurance.
- Undead: **Weakness to ichor siphoning**; ichor infusion empowers; umbra heals them like anima heals the living. Skeletons: no ichor to siphon; ichor ×2 empowerment, umbra ×2 healing.
- Metal gear takes normal umbra damage; draining can't break an item (next hit breaks it) unless it beats max durability. Once-living gear and stone resist. Anima heats metal armour (hurts wielder, counters freezing); siphoning anima cools it (cold damage).
- Defaults: no energy of that type → no effect; never-living matter can't hold anima or ichor.
# Progression (see tree-engine handoff)
- Level every session 1→50; 3 vocation + 3 attribute points per level; skill points from raising attributes; specialization points from investing in tree tracks (anima track → animist points). Achievements gate/grant/reward; visibility = next purchase only, saved per player across characters; respec costs a level.
# Vocation structure (9/28, Reece)
- **Sigils = everything that goes in a foci slot.** Shapes and modifiers are both sigils and use the same slot type. **Soul gems also fit sigil slots.** This is the Channeler's gear, equivalent to beast gear and living armor.
- **Spirits are sealed into gems on your gear** (in sigil slots).
- **Minor spirits** exist, equivalent to minor beasts and minor plants.
- **Specializations = energy types,** trained by befriending spirits that can manipulate that energy (wisps are high-anima spirits and train anima). Also combination specializations, rarer because their spirits are rarer: **aura (steam)**, **smoke**, **flareling**, **soulfire**, and more. Later: combining a third element.
- **Craft:** spell nails and spikes (the Channeler's alchemy).
- **Foci and sigils (9/28):** a foci is designed for **one function**; an object can carry **more than one foci**. You must push energy through a foci, so you need the energy first. **Sigils wear with use;** the material sets how long they last; some are single-use. **Sigils can be charges;** charges are consumed on use and some can be recharged. **Sigils are programmed:** each foci's sigils are set when bound and changed in downtime; they all fire on every cast. **The reversing foci** is a special, expensive foci: it siphons into an empty charge until the charge is full, then reverses and infuses until it's empty, then reverses again. Ordinary foci can't do this.
- **Siphon foci don't need energy up front** (only pushing energy does).
- **Foci materials (9/28):** material **kinds** set energy compatibility: **wood and bone → anima and ichor; gemstones and metal → aether and umbra.** Material **variants** within a kind set durability, time to craft, skill required to craft, and **bandwidth**.
- **Wear = total energy that travels through the foci** (bigger throughput, more wear); **each sigil's type adds wear on top;** sigils also limit bandwidth.
- **Sigils are programmed in order:** the spell travels sigil to sigil and only casts when it passes through the foci.
- **Area shapes: full effect per target,** energy paid per target.
- **Sigil quality sets a shape's numbers** (range, radius, target count): shapes are written as ranges, and the quality of sigils a Channeler can make grows with their skill.
- **When bandwidth cuts a spell short, the shortfall is split evenly across targets.** Example: 3 targets at 6 each needs 18; if 3 doesn't get through, each takes 5.
- **Two identical sigils in the same stage** (using two slots) run in parallel and **add their bandwidth.**
- Area siphon: area shapes can't siphon in the starting kit (unlocked later in the tree); siphon gain is capped by empty reserve; wear and bandwidth also limit it.
- **The foci itself is a sigil.** Foci **form** (Nail…Stave) sets die size; material **variant** sets bandwidth.
- **Each sigil has its own maximum throughput; the lowest caps the whole foci.**
- **Incompatible energy can't get through a sigil:** it damages the sigil instead (1 wear per point) until the energy is used up or the sigil breaks. If it breaks, the leftover energy continues into the spell, but the broken sigil has no effect.
- Exceeding a sigil's maximum throughput just caps the output.
- Future: special hybrid materials.
- **Energy works differently:** the reserve is a balance and a cap and doesn't deplete like ferality or Overgrowth. **A Channeler can and should start combat with a full reserve.**
- **Out of combat** (already the richest of the three): bindings and weavings, speaking with spirits, scouting, harvesting souls, sensing life force.
- **The hunting/foraging equivalent:** finding spirits and energy wells.
- **Vocation tree branches:** **Withering** (aether buildup, aether sickness, mummification, spirit form), Channeling, Spirit Weaving, Sigil Binding.
# Reactions revised (9/28, Reece)
- **Anima + umbra = soulfire.**
- **Aether + umbra = smoke.** Smoke obscures and raises Soul Resistance: soul energy channeled through a smoke cloud is resisted. **Smoke no longer evicts spirits.**
- **Smoke suppresses Endurance recovery:** take a wound inside smoke and Endurance refills to half, not full. (Petrification considered and dropped.)
- **Capturing spirits:** a Channeler can capture a defeated spirit in a gem, which unlocks that spirit for the next session.
- **Aura (steam) evicts weak spirits** but exposes those inside to strong ones.
- **Smoke + anima explosion: rejected** in favour of petrification.
- **Coatings** (coat things, including the ground):
	- **Umbra + ichor = Clot:** gummy crust. Ground: grabs (grapple). Armour/shield: temporary extra durability layer. Wound: stops bleeding.
	- **Clot + aether = Rot** (umbra + ichor + aether; three-energy reactions use three different energies) (three-energy combination): eats the durability of organic gear.
	- **Aether + ichor = Witch blood:** makes spirits tangible and injurable; makes coated armour and weapons effective against spirits; defends against psionics and other soul magic, including friendly magic (blocks everything).
- **Spirit companions must be willing.** Unwilling spirits can be captured but only become companions if befriended; then the normal ladder applies (a point per session they come with you).
- **All companions use a relationship ladder.** Vocational companions give specialization points; normal companions give skill points; some (e.g. the Page) give specialization points to a compatible vocation.
- A creature made of two elements isn't necessarily undergoing their reaction (a human doesn't always have an aura). "Death" and "undeath" were thinking exercises about creature makeup, not reactions.

### Personal bandwidth & soul sigils (9/28, Reece)
- **Accepted:** The Channeler is a foci. With skill they shape **soul sigils** from their own soul. A high-level Channeler can cast a high-bandwidth, multi-sigil spell with no foci.
- **Accepted:** Soul sigils can be placed **before** a foci's physical spell channel. This is the easiest way to modify a spell. A Channeler doesn't have to put any sigil between themselves and the foci.
- **Accepted:** **Everyone has a personal bandwidth** (probably Intuition-based; pending). Soul Resistance modifies it: **aura raises it, smoke lowers it.** A Channeler can raise or lower their own (allies' and enemies' pending).
- **Accepted:** Personal bandwidth caps both **output and input.** Going over it doesn't hurt you. Excess input can't heal you; it goes into the air as **nascent energy.** A high-skill Channeler can draw on nascent energy as a secondary source.
- **Accepted:** A foci buffers bandwidth load, especially with storage gems.
- **Accepted:** Foci bandwidth = die size × dice count + a number set by quality (details pending). The Channeler's own bandwidth still limits output and input.
- **Accepted:** A new Channeler with a stave performs about like one with a spike. Small foci trade power for versatility (e.g. 2 gloves + 2 wands + spikes on the belt; a spell-shield foci plus staff and gloves). **Tattoos** become an option as foci at higher levels: stronger, but hard to swap.
- **Accepted:** Aura and smoke affect sigils too. Staves can be built to work only inside aura, or to create the aura as part of the cast (high level).
- **Accepted:** Fields push each other. A growing field pushes back a still or shrinking one, and the bigger growth wins, so a field being cast usually beats an existing one. This gets complicated when both fields are being channeled. Example tactic: a three-stage spell that drops a small aura, fires a big spell, then drops a small smoke field.
- **Direction (accepted, details pending):** sigil order should matter in meaningful, experimental ways. Ideas: programming sigils; channels splitting into several foci for multi-part spells; repeat-firing to extend duration (e.g. an umbra bubble shield); choosing where a shape lands and how big; on-the-fly shape tuning at high skill.
- **Pending (Reece leaning yes):** Willpower and modifier bonuses count against bandwidth.
- **Parked:** creature Endurance/wound balance; a Hexicon battle calculator (average damage output → rounds to wipe each side).
### Bandwidth & chaining (9/28 late, Reece)
- **Accepted:** Roll + modifier is what uses bandwidth (bonuses count against it). A low-level caster pushes whatever they roll through the sigils and has to plan for overflow.
- **Accepted:** Energy from your reserve or body passes through your personal bandwidth. Gems, companion spirits and nascent energy feed the foci directly.
- **Accepted:** Foci bandwidth scales with the sources drawn (die size × sources + a quality number). It's still limited by bandwidth. Quality = crafter skill + material kind + variant.
- **Accepted:** Composition rule: **each shape delivers the spell to where the next shape begins** (a bolt lands, then triggers the next part of the channel). How each shape hands off when chained still needs to be worked out, e.g. where does a bolt fired after an explosion go?
- **Accepted:** Field pushing happens in turns. Each expansion pushes when it happens, in order.
- **Pending (Reece "maybe"):** Higher bandwidth lets you choose bigger dice / buy dice.
- **Parked, revised:** The Hexicon battle calculator must be **live**. It updates constantly as things change, measures how the fight is actually going, and uses average damage actually being dealt, not just potential damage.
### Shape expressions, triggers, contingencies (9/28 night, Reece)
- **Accepted:** The foci determines die size (reaffirmed). "Bandwidth buys dice" is dropped.
- **Accepted:** A spell channel can end in **two foci firing the same thing** to get an extra die.
- **Accepted:** Output overflow goes nascent into the air, the same as input overflow.
- **Accepted:** **Shape expressions**, chosen at sigil binding. Example: a bolt can pass its next effect **into** the target, pass **through** it, or **bounce** back from it. The handoff options (shrapnel outward, seek nearest, aim) are all valid expressions.
- **Accepted:** A **Tag shape** marks one or more targets to be hit by another spell in the same channel, or in adjacent channels of the same casting.
- **Accepted:** A **Trigger shape** activates an effect from the trigger point on a trigger type set at sigil binding (traps).
- **Accepted:** Needed: **Delay sigils**, **trigger sigils**, and targeting choices (**armor vs wielder, weapon vs wielder**).
- **Accepted:** A **spell countermeasure** that attracts spells to itself: an active defense siphon, which can itself be an armed spell waiting on its trigger.
- **Accepted (ideas):** **Death sigil** (fires if the caster dies); **Blood sigil** (fires when the caster, or the target, takes a wound). Examples: an ichor spell that waits until a creature drops below a threshold, then siphons a heal back to you; an anima flashbang flare that fires when you're wounded; an umbra sphere that fires on your wound and pushes physical enemies away.

- **Pending (Claude):** Split into a **Trigger shape** (spatial: a point or area that waits) versus **condition sigils** (Blood, Death, Threshold; they gate everything after them in the channel).
- **Pending (Claude):** An extra die from two foci still passes through the shared upstream sigils, so it costs double bandwidth, anima and wear there.
