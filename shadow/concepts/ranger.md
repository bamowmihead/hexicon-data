# Ranger

**Vocation · active build target.** Cultivation-based wilderness vocation; faction pairing: Herbalists.

**Locked canon (1.0, live):** Growth curve deliberately slowed; attunement-as-process applies — see [[concepts/attunement]]. Shares **Reagents** with [[concepts/berserker]] as vocation energy (~Intuition-linked; overuse = control loss — from specs, design intent).

**Superseded by 2.0 (designed 9/27–9/28, NOT released — ships all at once):** the energy is **Overgrowth**, produced by your plants every round, cap Intuition × 10 + 20; the body roots in place and acts through **root clones**; the companion is **living armor** grown as a symbiote; specialization is by **growth habit** (Trees & Bushes, Vines, Fungi, Mold, Algae, Lichen, Moss); the garden grows by companion planting. The whole ruling set: [[concepts/ranger-2-0-design]].

**Historical frame (March omnibus — concept canon, numbers unconfirmed):** Cultivation ladder mirroring trances; 9 starter cultivation plants with complete mechanics; plant-bond progression.

**In the app:** the vocation row [[vocations/ranger]]; 11 cultivation cards and the Seed Bag (1.0); the 2.0 tables — plant cards, plant levels, grown plants, root clones, gardens, garden plots, plant complements, plant biomes (migs 383–386) — exist as data under [[plants]], nothing of 2.0 is live.

**Open:** how cultivation expresses in the Effects Engine (same vocation-entity-frame question as trances); cross-breeding plants (parked — Reece likes it; hard to design).

**Sources:** `library-omnibus-2026-03-27.md` · Ranger Guide 2.0 + Cultivation Cards (April play docs, stale frame) · `library-current-state §7/§14`
