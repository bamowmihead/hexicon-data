# Attunement

**Concept page — referenced by [[concepts/berserker]], [[concepts/ranger]], [[systems/07-species-and-bestiary]], [[systems/06-vocations-and-progression]].**

**Definition (locked 6/06, Reece verbatim distilled):** Attunement is an intentional, slow form of growth — a process, not a purchase. Three paths raise attunement with a creature (1.0):

1. **Taming it and deepening the bond**
2. **Hunting it and subsuming its soul**
3. **Wearing associated beast gear**

**2.0 (designed 9/27, not released):** attunement is **befriend, never kill** — Speech (at peace) → Aspect form (a favour) → Quest (it goes on expedition with you; an attunement point) → a point every session it survives with you at peace; points are spent on **that creature's attunement tree** (Were / Wild / Bond / Beast gear). 1 attunement point = 1 body-plan point. The Ranger's mirror is the **plant relationship ladder** (find → forage + alchemy + reagents → symbiosis → a point per session). See [[concepts/berserker-2-0-design]], [[concepts/ranger-2-0-design]].

**Interactions:** Body-type specialization raises attunement with ALL creatures matching that body type — in 2.0 the kingdoms are retired and **body plans** are the specialization, earned by improving attunements. Animal companions = the slower, less-limited trance-learning path. Burn vs Subsume (1.0): subsuming feeds attunement permanently; burning spends the soul instead — **retired in 2.0**.

**Open:** attunement measurement/levels in play (the 2.0 ladder answers the shape; the numbers are open); how the Effects Engine represents attunement state (vocation entity frame question).
