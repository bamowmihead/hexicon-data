# 10 · Companions, Houses & PCs

**What:** PCs, Houses, companions as first-class app entities. House = per-player vault + login identity. Companions = the retinue layer (founding why: embodied hobbies solving player buy-in — now optional flavor).

**Current state:** Uniform companion leveling LOCKED. **House/Company storehouses shipped** — first-class container kinds with hard caps (house 500 / company 2000 lb), currency weighs, C&M trackers, sell-at-half, access gated to staging-or-Hub. Companions and **vehicles** are owned by House/Company (`owner_kind/owner_id`). **Player/device app fully shipped** — the real desktop inventory over LAN, end-turn, **Renovations from the device**, PIN change, theme/display settings that roam with the House login. Companies carry **per-company map knowledge** (map fog-of-war scopes to the logged-in House's company; mig 178). **Renovations** (`holding_upgrades`): 17 company + draft house upgrades, spend the holding's own salt. Companions are an alternate slower trance-learning path.

**Since (Claude's summary at migration, 2026-10-07, not canon):** companion kinds, ladders, profiles and bonds exist as tables (mig 377); the Berserker gets one free beast companion and the Ranger one free living armor in the 2.0 designs; **Bledger died in the session of 09-18** and is memorialised on his House and Company (mig 375; see [[memorials]]); a dead PC's root items move to the House.

**Open decisions:** retinue economic tiers + Charm-gated size; retirement/prestige layer (canon, shape open); between-session occupations (parked).

**Waiting on Reece:** retinue specifics + the downtime companion-assignment list (who-does-what during a rest; see handoff 6/12).
