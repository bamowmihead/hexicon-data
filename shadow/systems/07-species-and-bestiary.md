# 07 · Species & Bestiary

**What:** Bestiary = creatures + humanoid NPC templates; the species framework above it.

**Current state:** Batkin locked (Batfolk deprecated). **Natural Undeath** locked (6/07; closes the "plain undead" rename task): undeath is the DEFAULT fate of bodies unless burned — buried→mycelia zombie, left out→virus zombie, terrible death→remnant (vector undesigned), umbral infusion→husk. Husks = NEW life, no memories; decades of context → sentience; eventual playable species. Nekromech = Nek'ra piloting (neither). Nek'ra March arc canon (fallen silvan oppressors → necromech privateers → playable origin). Silvan tree-birth canon. Goblinoid spawning pools canon-deep (skull-return cycle, bezoar core — roots to 2024 Hordekin). **Method principle:** taxonomy is frame-relative; Primal/Chaos/Construct trichotomy (2024) is a tool, not THE framework — iteration is deliberate. Bestiary held **176 creature types** (DB 8/23, schema 333) — **+34 in the 08-17→08-22 week, the first bestiary authoring since the 6/26 character-book import** (which brought it to 142; those stats remain redline-me drafts, not canon). The new rows are **Batkin, built out as a people**: six base kinds by feeding strategy — Carni, Vampiric, Tent-maker, Insectivore, Fishing, Fruit — each with 2–4 role variants (Tank / Long Spear / Blunderbuss · Assassin / Grappler / Thrower · Artillery / Trap Ambusher / Mounted Gun · Commander / Rifle / Slinger / Spear & Buckler · Bomber / Anti-air / Dropper / Sniper · Carrier / Battle). Also **Ork + 5 variants** (Pack Leader / Pyromancer / Piker / Berserker / Sniper), **Warg**, two Black Company units (Crossbowman, Pikeman), **Newt**, and the four **faction Reps** (Guild / Church / Academy / Circle). Retinue templates 17 / 70 members, incl. **Ork Warband** and **Batkin Flight**. New `tags` vocabulary: `Person` — "a people, not an animal; cannot be field dressed or butchered" — applied to the batkin, so who is butcherable is now data. The snapshot of 2026-10-07 holds **190** creature types.

**RULED 08-24 (Reece): the batkin are an explosives + firearms faction, and they are the opposition for the 09-04 session.** That retro-explains the 08-17→08-22 roster — Blunderbuss, Artillery, Mounted Gun, Bomber, Anti-air, Dropper are a gunpowder army, not flavour. Supporting datum in the DB: `sense_kinds` records **19 batkin already carry echolocation at 30 m** (catalogued for vocabulary; the senses engine resolves no signature for it yet — [[systems/04-combat-and-resolution]]).

**Soul kinds (2026-09-28, Reece's ruling, mig 370–374):** undead / Nek'ra → undead; dwarven constructs → inert; other constructs → elemental; everything else living. Each creature row carries `soul_kind`.

**Note (Claude's read, not canon):** the batkin were authored as one row per variant, exactly the manual path the parked *Creature Modifier System* entry says is fine for now — 34 rows in one week is the first real pressure on that decision.

**🔨 PARKED SESSIONS:** species-axis reconciliation; goblin tribes merge (March five-tribes × April adaptation-strategies; includes Onos — ancestors found: Shield brutes + Goblin Wurms).

**Waiting on Reece:** both parked sessions; body plans (shared with Inventory).
