# 05 · Magic & the Artes

**What:** Seven Artes taxonomy (Ars Mentis/Animae/Mundi/Ordo/Liminis/Chronis/Deus) + conjugation grammar (root = medium, suffix = modality). Decade-strong — LOCKED. Roots: Crater's "The Magics" triad (2024) → the Nine's creation acts (2019).

**Current state:** Primal/umbral essence canon since Sept 2024. **Husking** (canon): umbral infusion creating a husk — undead construct powered by umbral energy + caster's intention/command. Foci-gem targeting shapes (Bolt/Channel/Contact/Shape/Area/Emanate/Blast/Wave) = direct ancestor of Effects Target vocab.

**✅ POLARITY SWAP LOCKED (6/07 Vision Room):** primal = raw/powerful/quick; umbral = steady/enduring — "Primal burns hot and fast; umbral burns slow and forever." Decay chain canon: corpses are batteries of power; expiring primal becomes umbral; umbral infusion → husk. Soul framework canon: "a brain is a vessel for a soul" — essence IS soul-stuff; telepathy = souls communing. Ripple pass still owed: runewriting, mutations, Burn flavor, undead metaphysics, Sept-2024 origin texts.

**Superseded in part (2026-09-27):** the four-state soul-energy cosmology — anima / aether / ichor / umbra on two axes — supersedes the 6/07 "expiring primal becomes umbral" line, which predates the living/dead split. The whole ruling set lives in [[concepts/soul-energy-and-channeler-design-canon-2026-09-27]]; the Channeler vocation built from it shipped in Hexicon on 2026-09-28 → 10-05 (migs 368–375).
