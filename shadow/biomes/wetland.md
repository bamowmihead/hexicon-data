## In his words

**description:** **Sound:** The Wetlands are silent day & night — only moving water, and an occasional rustle in brush or splash in the water. **Smell:** A strong sulfuric smell occasionally finds its way to you here. **Color:** Dark brown muddy waters with pale green & yellow moss which creates a shelf around waterholes. An occasional dark green willow tree. **Light:** Bright in the day and in the night. The water is lit up by glowing plant life in the night making it easier to hunt aquatic creatures. **Willow Trees** — Dominant canopy tree of the wetlands. **Fairy Light Trees** — Dandelion-like seed packets glow as the wind takes them from the tree. The air sparkles with them in the night when the wind picks up. **Berry Bushes:** Blueberries, cranberries, & huckleberries. **Berry Vines:** Blackberries, grapevine, & ginger creeper. **Man-Eater Moss** — Pale green & yellow squishy thick strands up to a foot long. Easy to slip on. Creates a shelf at the shore of waterholes. Can be hard to get out of the water when this moss is present. **Cattails** **Lily Pads** **Glow Vines** — Grape-size glowing fruits glow only in the night. Sneaking in water at night is harder but hunting aquatic life is much easier. **Splinter Pods** — Large white pods the size of a human head grow on dead trees & corpses. Extract bones & wood splinters into their structure. If touched they explode like a shrapnel grenade. If ignited the explosion is bigger. Small pods can be harvested as throwable weapons. Extraction is very dangerous. **Ember Lichen** — Kills trees, consumes them, then sets them on fire so their seed will spread. The oil they produce can be turned into a quickly burning weapon coating.
