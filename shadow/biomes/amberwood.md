## In his words

**description:** **Sound:** Constant rustling in the bushes and understory trees day & night. Chirps & clicks only in the day. The leaves here are particularly crunchy. **Smell:** The air is still and dusty. Dust gets worse around leaf piles which decompose into it. If kicked up too much it can become hard to breathe. **Color:** Occasional canopy tree with dark green leaves and an occasional amber-leaved tree. Understory rhododendrons have dark green leaves and ground underneath is covered in crunchy leaves. **Light:** Light cuts through the canopy easily. Under canopy trees is dark green moss. **Verticality:** An occasional climbable tree. **Young Amber Fir Trees** — Amber to orange colored leaves. Resin leaks from trunk to tip. They are so prevalent they can be used to climb. Resin heads harden quickly into amber. Young resin heads have hard shells filled with delicious syrupy resin — valuable as alchemical base & restores endurance. Old amber heads glow in the night. **Young Verdant Fir Trees** — Similar to amber cousins but produce little resin. Die younger. Often hollowed out while living. When blown down they become wildlife habitat. **Dust Devil Trees** — Short understory trees similar to rhododendron. Leaves decompose slowly causing buildup. When disturbed, leaf piles release massive dust — obscuring and hard to breathe. Dust is highly flammable. **Hollytrop Bush** — Poisonous berries and long tough thorns that stay sharp after decomposing. Ground near them is thick with natural caltrops. Collectible for personal use. **Yellow Fern** — Grow under fir trees, very densely and tall under amber trees. Waist-high. **Fish Caps** — Small brown mushrooms that smell like fish when ripe. Attracts cattlepos which will fight for them. Usable as bait for predators and for taming. **Leanda's Hair** — Strange lichen that looks like hair. Grows densely on Verdant Firs, never on Amber Firs. Great for making durable cordage or rope. **Pig Pods** — Strange orchids near Amber Firs that tap resin and excrete it with a powerful hallucinogenic toxin. Pods are just big enough for pygmy hogs. A pod consuming a hog has valuable alchemical elements.
