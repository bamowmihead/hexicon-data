## In his words

**description:** **Sound:** Howls, chirps, rattles, & clicks ever present in the day. Dead silent at night — only the wind. **Smell:** Fresh air with a consistent cool breeze under the tall canopy. **Color:** Deep dark greens with occasional amber-leaved trees. Green ferns up to your knees on the ground. **Light:** Large canopy blocks light effectively. Moonlight is not enough to light your path at night. **Verticality:** Large trees can easily be climbed here. **Old Amber Firs** — Shadow their younger versions. Amber and resin heads grow larger and glow brighter. Thick wide canopy blots out sunlight. Amber head glow visible in cloudy weather during day. **Old Verdant Firs** — Monstrous trees with thick dark green canopies. Slightly shorter than amber counterpart. Leanda's Hair grows thicker and longer. Very easy to climb. Often have hovels hidden within. Oldest and thickest could hold a spiral staircase. Sometimes contain old Gnomish homes. Sometimes have entrances into the undergrowth below. **Pillow Moss** — Thick plushy dark green moss grows almost everywhere. So soft it's a little hard to walk on. Easy to sleep on without a bedroll. **Banana Fern** — Large yellow ferns as tall as rhododendron trees. Rain, loud sounds, and darkness cause them to roll up. Seed pods are yellow, sweet like bananas, very filling and nutritious. **Weeping Vines** — Pale green-blue vines hanging from Verdant Firs. More water in aquifer = more dense. Can create vine forests dozens of feet deep. Constantly release water drops creating ponds underneath. An oasis — the whole forest comes to drink. **Coal Shroom** — Large black mushrooms that look scorched. Grow on Verdant Firs. Hard like wood. Can burn and hold ember for days. Makes starting fires in rain easier. **Slick Spots** — White lichen that grows in place of pillow moss near weeping vines. Very slippery.
